Aside
  • Open with Ctrl Shift / or the Aside tab. One aside per thread; it persists with the thread.
  • Runs with read-only tools on a provider that offers an isolated, tool-less run. Providers without one are absent from its picker, named in a tooltip.
  • Defaults to the smaller model of the thread's provider, so asides don't spend the main model's limit.
  • In compact and glance panes the tab opens over the maximised thread, like the other right-panel tabs.
Second opinion
  • From a turn's action row: pick a provider and model other than the thread's. The review gets the turn and its diff, read-only.
  • The card sits under the turn it reviewed and is not part of the thread's context until you press Send to thread.
  • Send to thread queues the review as your next message, so it obeys the same queue, steer and interrupt rules.
