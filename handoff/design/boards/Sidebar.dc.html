Sidebar · refined

One sidebar, one density, organised by grids: workspace → grid → threads in pane order. Each thread is a two-line card: title and state, then branch, PR and provider. Close and Hold keep grids clean, multi-select handles many at once, and Ctrl . finds what needs you.

1Stable order

Threads in a grid follow the panes: left to right, top to bottom. Activity never moves a card; only its state and weight change.

Titles are semibold while the thread is Ready, Needs you or Back and you have not opened it; read threads settle to secondary ink.

2What is on screen

The open grid’s name reads in full contrast; the focused thread is a raised card. Nothing else marks “on screen”.

3Find what needs you
Ctrl . next · Ctrl Shift . previous

Ctrl . jumps to the next Needs you or Ready thread, in any workspace: it opens its grid and focuses the pane. This replaces an inbox list.

4Quick actions on hover

Hover swaps the state for Hold (clock) and Close. Keyboard: H opens Hold on the focused card. Close never asks; it shows Undo.

5On hold and Closed

As upstream, the rail ends with two shelves for every workspace. On hold lists held threads by wake time; Closed lists closed threads newest first, with Show more for older ones. Each opens in place with the same cards; dropping a card on Closed closes it. A thread comes back to the grid and slot it left; if that grid is gone or holds six, Houston opens a new grid in the same workspace, and the card reads Back until you open it.

6Multi-select

2 threads closedCtrl Z

Shift-click selects a range, Ctrl-click adds one. Selected cards take the accent wash. The bar replaces the rail foot while a selection exists; Esc clears it.

7Pull request on the thread
#412 merged · thread closed

The PR number sits on the branch line while the thread has an open pull request. When it merges, the thread closes itself (Auto-close, per thread) and a toast offers Undo.

8Drag with intent
Drop to make a new grid

Drag a thread inside its grid to reorder it (the pane moves with it). Drop on another grid to move it there. Drop on a workspace name to start a new grid with it.

9Search filters in place
provEsc

Typing filters workspaces, grids and threads in the tree, with the match highlighted. Enter opens the first result; Esc clears and restores the tree as it was.

Long titles truncate on one line. Resting on a card for a moment shows the full title, workspace, branch and model. The rail resizes from 280 to 440px and remembers its width.

11Hide a grid

Hide a grid from the sidebar without closing its threads. Hidden grids are listed in the workspace menu under “Show hidden grids (1)”; Ctrl . still reaches their threads.

12New thread, new grid

Hovering a grid shows +: it starts a thread in that grid, in the next free pane. A workspace without grids offers New grid.

13Changes on the workspace

Uncommitted lines added and removed across the workspace, in mono. Clicking it opens Changes.
